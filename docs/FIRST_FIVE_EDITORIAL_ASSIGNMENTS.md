# RevOps Pulse — First five editorial briefing assignments
Status: RESEARCH ASSIGNMENTS ONLY. No facts verified and nothing approved for publication.

Each article requires at least one primary source and an independent corroborating source where available. Record source URL, date, exact claim supported, and reviewer sign-off in Editorial Studio. Do not invent data or assert claims based only on RSS snippets.

## 1. CRM / Revenue Operations
**Working title:** Are CRM AI copilots improving pipeline quality—or merely data entry?
**Core question:** Which CRM AI capabilities measurably improve forecast quality, opportunity hygiene and sales-manager decisions?
**Research:** Vendor release documentation, independent deployment studies, measured before/after adoption outcomes.
**Reader deliverable:** A decision checklist for a 30-day pilot: missing fields, forecast variance, rep adoption and manager intervention.

## 2. GTM / AI
**Working title:** Where should revenue teams allow AI agents to act without approval?
**Core question:** Which automated sales tasks are low-risk enough for autonomous execution, and which require human review?
**Research:** Enterprise AI governance documentation, published vendor product limits, independently documented failure modes.
**Reader deliverable:** Action-by-risk matrix for research, lead enrichment, outreach, CRM edits and commercial commitments.

## 3. RevOps / Reporting
**Working title:** Pipeline coverage is not pipeline health
**Core question:** Which measurements uncover weak conversion, deal slippage and data-quality issues that headline pipeline multiples obscure?
**Research:** Credible definitions and methodologies; distinguish hypothetical examples from measured benchmarks.
**Reader deliverable:** Weekly dashboard specification for coverage, stage conversion, age, slippage and forecast calibration.

## 4. GTM / Buying Journeys
**Working title:** What changed in enterprise software buying—and how should GTM teams respond?
**Core question:** What verified recent evidence supports changes in buying-group composition, procurement timelines and channel influence?
**Research:** Dated primary market surveys, methods/sample sizes, independent research and contrary findings.
**Reader deliverable:** Proposed adjustments to stakeholder mapping, deal inspection and buying-committee enablement.

## 5. Customer Success / Revenue Expansion
**Working title:** Expansion revenue: signals worth trusting before renewal
**Core question:** Which product-usage and stakeholder indicators correlate with expansion readiness, and which are misleading?
**Research:** Independent CS research, vendor feature documentation and clear causal caveats.
**Reader deliverable:** Practical account review scorecard for adoption, outcomes, champions, service risk and expansion intent.

## Publication gate for each
- Confirm meaningful new information and date, not simply a generic evergreen essay.
- Verify material facts directly from cited source(s), with editor's working notes.
- Make the distinction between factual observation, interpretation and recommendation explicit.
- Original headline, prose and analysis; no reproduction of publisher summaries/images without permission.
- RevOps applicability demonstrated through a decision, metric or operational implication.
- Approval checkboxes and named human editor completed.
- Only then import into public/approved-intelligence.json and publish through reviewed PR.
